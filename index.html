How it works

Four steps, in about two weeks

Every finding is checked by a person before it reaches your report.

  1. Scope and deposit

    We agree which page types to test. Half the fee starts the work.

  2. Automated and keyboard testing

    Open-source scanners run on every page type, then a full keyboard walk-through of the path to checkout.

  3. Screen-reader testing

    The main pages and checkout are tested with NVDA and VoiceOver, each draft finding is confirmed or rejected, and the report is signed off.

  4. Report and re-test

    You get the report and an issue spreadsheet. Once your developer has fixed the issues, the fixed items are re-tested once, within 30 days.

If you have a claim

The technical part, alongside your attorney

This is not legal advice. The audit is a technical assessment of your site at a point in time. If you have a claim or a demand letter, talk to your attorney first; I can work with them or with you, whichever they prefer.

A site is never called "ADA compliant" or "certified" here, because no such certification exists, and no audit can promise how a claim ends. What it does give you is a clear list of what to fix, a re-test of the fixes, and a dated record of the testing.

No overlays or widgets: the fixes go into your site's own code.

Questions

Common questions

What is WCAG?

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, published by the W3C. Level AA of WCAG 2.1 or 2.2 is the level most often used to judge whether a website works for people with disabilities, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or zoom.

Will the audit make my site ADA compliant?

No audit can promise that, and there is no official ADA certification for websites. The audit shows exactly what to fix against WCAG 2.1 and 2.2 Level AA, and the re-test checks the fixes. Legal questions are for your attorney.

Isn't an automated scanner enough?

Scanners catch only part of the problems. Many barriers, such as a checkout a keyboard user can't finish or a button a screen reader announces without a name, only show up when a person tests. That's why every audit includes keyboard and screen-reader testing.

How long does it take?

About two weeks from the deposit to the report. The re-test of the fixed items happens within 30 days of delivery, when your developer is ready.

Can you work with our developer or agency?

Yes. The report is written for them, and fix guidance by email is part of the monthly plan. Agencies can also order audits for their clients.
